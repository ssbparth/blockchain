The Flow

How VeriChain works

From wallet to on-chain asset in 4 simple steps.

1
Connect Wallet
User connects their Ethereum wallet. A cryptographic DID is created — no usernames, no passwords.
2
Create Identity
Profile metadata is pinned to IPFS. The IPFS URI is registered on the smart contract — permanently and immutably.
3
Mint Assets
Admins mint NFT-based assets (gold, certificates, stocks) to user wallets. Every mint creates an on-chain event.
4
Verify Instantly
Anyone can verify ownership by querying the smart contract — no middleman, no delay, no doubt.
